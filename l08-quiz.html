#Mock Quiz 8A

Question 1 (3 marks)
  1. With 5 bins per feature, how many cells does a 6-feature space have? (1 mark)
  2. Points are uniform in the unit ball of R20\mathbb{R}^{20}R20. What fraction lies within ϵ=0.1\epsilon=0.1ϵ=0.1 of the surface? (1 mark)
  3. Why does this hurt the kkk-nearest-neighbour classifier? (1 mark)
Solution & marking
  1. 56=15,6255^6=\mathbf{15{,}625}56=15,625 cells.
  2. 1−(1−ϵ)d=1−0.920=1−0.1216≈0.8781-(1-\epsilon)^d=1-0.9^{20}=1-0.1216\approx\mathbf{0.878}1−(1−ϵ)d=1−0.920=1−0.1216≈0.878 — 88% of the volume is in the thin outer shell.
  3. In high dimensions almost every point is near the surface and at nearly the same distance from a test point, and the cells are mostly empty, so the "nearest" neighbours are not actually close or similar.

#Mock Quiz 8B

Question 1 (3 marks)

True or False, with a one-line reason:

  1. PCA needs the class labels of the data.
  2. The features produced by PCA are uncorrelated.
  3. Dividing the covariance matrix by n−1n-1n−1 instead of nnn changes the principal components.
Solution & marking
  1. False. PCA is unsupervised: it uses only the spread (covariance) of the data — which is its main limitation for classification (use LDA for that).
  2. True. Cov⁡(z)=W⊤CW=diag⁡(λ1,…,λk)\operatorname{Cov}(z)=W^\top CW=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_k)Cov(z)=W⊤CW=diag(λ1​,…,λk​), because the eigenvectors are orthogonal.
  3. False. It multiplies every eigenvalue by the same factor n/(n−1)n/(n-1)n/(n−1); the eigenvectors (and the fractions TkT_kTk​) are unchanged.

Question 1 (4 marks)

A centred data matrix S∈R2×5S\in\mathbb{R}^{2\times5}S∈R2×5 (samples as columns) has singular values 555 and 111.
