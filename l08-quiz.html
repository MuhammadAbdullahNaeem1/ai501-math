#Mock Quiz 8B

Question 1 (3 marks)

True or False, with a one-line reason:

  1. PCA needs the class labels of the data.
  2. The features produced by PCA are uncorrelated.
  3. Dividing the covariance matrix by n−1n-1n−1 instead of nnn changes the principal components.
Solution & marking
  1. False — PCA uses only the spread of the data; that is exactly its limitation for classification (use LDA).
  2. True — Cov⁡(z)=W⊤CW=diag⁡(λ1,…,λk)\operatorname{Cov}(z)=W^\top CW=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_k)Cov(z)=W⊤CW=diag(λ1​,…,λk​) because the eigenvectors are orthogonal.
  3. False — every eigenvalue is scaled by the same factor; the eigenvectors and TkT_kTk​ do not change.
