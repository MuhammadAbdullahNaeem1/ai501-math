# Mid cheat sheet — 2 A4 sheets, 4 sides

Every formula and key point from L00–L06, plus the exam procedures (2×2 inverse, symmetric eigenvectors, Gram–Schmidt, complete solution, least squares/ridge/lasso, kNN, metrics) and the usual MCQ traps, packed into **4 A4 sides in two columns**.

:::warn The exam allows hand-written sheets only
The official instructions say *two hand-written, two-sided A4 sheets*. Treat the PDF as a **template**: print it to check the layout, then copy it by hand onto your own two sheets (writing it out is good revision too). Don't bring the printout into the hall.
:::

:::note How to use it
Side 1: L00 + vectors, norms, angles/statistics, span/GS/subspaces, matrix basics. Side 2: products, systems, inverses, determinants, pseudo-inverses, rank, column/null space, supervised learning, kNN. Side 3: metrics, regression, polynomial fits, ridge/lasso, eigen. Side 4: PSD/SVD preview, worked procedures, traps, true/false facts.
:::
