Mock Mid 4 — the official 2026 format

This paper follows the instructions sheet for the 2026 mid exactly: 3 hours in total. Part I: 30 MCQs worth 3 points each (90 points, 40% of the exam), to be attempted in the first 60 minutes. Part II: 7 problems worth 30 points in total (60% of the exam), in the remaining 120 minutes. Closed book; two hand-written two-sided A4 sheets and a calculator allowed.

Exam pattern: What the instructor said to be comfortable with

Inverting a 2×22\times22×2 matrix by hand, and finding the eigenvectors of a symmetric matrix once its eigenvalues are known. No rigorous proofs and no tedious steps; the Hessian is not examined (it comes in Week 10). Show your steps — "solutions with inadequate justifications and/or steps may not receive full credit".

Exam pattern: Part I rules

Select ALL (up to three) correct choices. Every incorrect choice carries a penalty of 111 point, but no question scores below zero. A question with kkk correct options awards 3/k3/k3/k points per correct tick.
