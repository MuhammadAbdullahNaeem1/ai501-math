Question 1 (3 marks)

True or False, with a one-line justification:

  1. The singular values of AAA are the square roots of the eigenvalues of A⊤AA^\top AA⊤A.
  2. The first principal component is the eigenvector of the covariance matrix with the smallest eigenvalue.
  3. The Hessian of f(x,y)=x2−y2f(x,y)=x^2-y^2f(x,y)=x2−y2 at the origin is positive definite.
Solution & marking
  1. True — A⊤A=V(Σ⊤Σ)V⊤A^\top A=V(\Sigma^\top\Sigma)V^\topA⊤A=V(Σ⊤Σ)V⊤, so its eigenvalues are σi2\sigma_i^2σi2​.
