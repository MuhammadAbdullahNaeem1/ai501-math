Question 1 (3 marks)

Decide whether each statement is true or false and justify your answer (half a mark for each).

  1. If A∈Rm×nA\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}A∈Rm×n with m<nm<nm<n, then N(A)≠{0}\mathcal N(A)\neq\{\mathbf 0\}N(A)={0}.
  2. If rank⁡(A)<n\operatorname{rank}(A)<nrank(A)<n, then Ax=bAx=bAx=b has infinitely many solutions.
  3. rank⁡(A⊤A)=rank⁡(A)\operatorname{rank}(A^\top A)=\operatorname{rank}(A)rank(A⊤A)=rank(A).
Solution & marking
  1. True. The rank is at most m<nm<nm<n, so by rank–nullity the nullity is n−rank⁡A≥n−m>0n-\operatorname{rank}A\ge n-m>0n−rankA≥n−m>0.
  2. False. There are either no solutions or infinitely many. For example, A=(1111)A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}A=(11​11​) with b=(1,2)b=(1,2)b=(1,2) has no solution.
  3. True. If A⊤Ax=0A^\top Ax=\mathbf 0A⊤Ax=0, then x⊤A⊤Ax=∥Ax∥2=0x^\top A^\top Ax=\|Ax\|^2=0x⊤A⊤Ax=∥Ax∥2=0, so Ax=0Ax=\mathbf 0Ax=0; conversely Ax=0Ax=\mathbf 0Ax=0 implies A⊤Ax=0A^\top Ax=\mathbf 0A⊤Ax=0. Hence N(A⊤A)=N(A)\mathcal N(A^\top A)=\mathcal N(A)N(A⊤A)=N(A), and since both matrices have nnn columns, rank–nullity gives them equal ranks.

Question 3 (4 marks)

A data matrix X∈R100×4X\in\mathbb{R}^{100\times4}X∈R100×4 has columns height (cm), weight (kg), height (inches) and age.

  1. What is the largest possible rank of XXX, and why? (1 mark)
  2. Give a non-zero vector in N(X)\mathcal N(X)N(X). (1 mark)
  3. Explain why the least-squares weights θ\thetaθ are not unique, and why X⊤XX^\top XX⊤X is singular. (2 marks)
Solution & marking
  1. Three. Height in inches is 12.54\tfrac{1}{2.54}2.541​ times height in centimetres, so at most three columns are independent.
  2. z=(1,0,−2.54,0)z=(1,0,-2.54,0)z=(1,0,−2.54,0), since Xz=(height in cm)−2.54 (height in inches)=0Xz=(\text{height in cm})-2.54\,(\text{height in inches})=\mathbf 0Xz=(height in cm)−2.54(height in inches)=0.
  3. For every ttt, the weights θ\thetaθ and θ+tz\theta+tzθ+tz give identical predictions Xθ=X(θ+tz)X\theta=X(\theta+tz)Xθ=X(θ+tz), so the loss cannot distinguish between them. Remember

For rank–nullity questions: (1) eliminate in matrix form; (2) count the pivots to get the rank; (3) use the free columns to get the nullity and the special solutions; (4) read solvability from the zero rows of [ A∣b ][\,A\mid b\,][A∣b].
