Mock Mid 1

The format copies the 2024 and 2025 AI 501 mid exams. Part I: 45 minutes, 20 MCQs worth 4 points each (80 points). Part II: 100 minutes, 8 problems (40 points). Sit it in one go, closed book, with a calculator. Solutions are folded under each question.

Exam pattern: Part I instructions (as on the real paper)

Select ALL (up to three) correct choices. Each correct tick earns credit; every incorrect tick costs 1 point, but no question goes below zero. To score an MCQ out of 4, award 4/(number of correct options)4/(\text{number of correct options})4/(number of correct options) for each correct tick and subtract 111 for each wrong one.

MCQ 1

Let u,v∈Rnu,v\in\mathbb{R}^nu,v∈Rn be unit vectors. Which statements are true?

  1. ∥u−v∥22=2−2u⊤v\|u-v\|_2^2=2-2u^\top v∥u−v∥22​=2−2u⊤v
  2. ∣u⊤v∣≤1|u^\top v|\le1∣u⊤v∣≤1
  3. u⊤v=1u^\top v=1u⊤v=1 implies u=vu=vu=v
  4. ∥u+v∥2=2\|u+v\|_2=2∥u+v∥2​=2 always
Solution & marking

(a), (b), (c). Expanding gives (a). Cauchy–Schwarz gives (b). Equality in Cauchy–Schwarz with a positive sign, for unit vectors, gives (c). Statement (d) is false: for orthogonal unit vectors, ∥u+v∥2=2\|u+v\|_2=\sqrt2∥u+v∥2​=2​.
