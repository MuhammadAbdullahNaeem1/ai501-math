#Mock Quiz 7A

Question 1 (3 marks)

True or False? Give a one-line justification for each.

  1. Every real matrix, of any shape, has a singular value decomposition.
  2. The singular values of a symmetric matrix are its eigenvalues.
  3. If A∈R5×3A\in\mathbb{R}^{5\times3}A∈R5×3 has rank 3, then AA⊤AA^\topAA⊤ is positive definite.
Solution & marking
  1. True — the SVD exists for every A∈Rm×nA\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}A∈Rm×n; that is its advantage over the eigen-decomposition.
  2. False — they are the absolute values of the eigenvalues: diag⁡(3,−2)\operatorname{diag}(3,-2)diag(3,−2) has singular values 333 and 222.
  3. False — AA⊤AA^\topAA⊤ is 5×55\times55×5 with rank 3, so it has 5−3=25-3=25−3=2 zero eigenvalues: PSD, not PD.

1 mark each: ½ for the verdict, ½ for the justification.
