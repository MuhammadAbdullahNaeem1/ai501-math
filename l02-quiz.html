Question 1 (4 marks)

True or False? If False, give a counter-example or counter-statement.
(a) A tall matrix A∈Rm×nA\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}A∈Rm×n, m>nm>nm>n, can have a right inverse.
(b) If AAA is square and XA=IXA=IXA=I, then AX=IAX=IAX=I.
(c) tr⁡(AB)=tr⁡(BA)\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)tr(AB)=tr(BA) for any A∈Rm×nA\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}A∈Rm×n, B∈Rn×mB\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times m}B∈Rn×m.
(d) If AAA and BBB are symmetric then ABABAB is symmetric.

Solution & marking

(a) False. A right inverse needs independent rows, but m>nm>nm>n rows in Rn\mathbb{R}^nRn are dependent. E.g. A=(10)A=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}A=(10​): AXAXAX is 2×22\times22×2 with second row 0, never I2I_2I2​.
(b) True. Square + left-invertible ⇒ independent columns ⇒ invertible, and by X=X(AY)=(XA)Y=YX=X(AY)=(XA)Y=YX=X(AY)=(XA)Y=Y the left inverse is the inverse.
(c) True. tr⁡(AB)=∑i∑kAikBki=∑k∑iBkiAik=tr⁡(BA)\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\sum_i\sum_k A_{ik}B_{ki}=\sum_k\sum_i B_{ki}A_{ik}=\operatorname{tr}(BA)tr(AB)=∑i​∑k​Aik​Bki​=∑k​∑i​Bki​Aik​=tr(BA).
(d) False. A=(1000)A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}A=(10​00​), B=(0110)B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}B=(01​10​) give AB=(0100)AB=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}AB=(00​10​). True only when AB=BAAB=BAAB=BA.
1 mark each: 0.5 verdict, 0.5 justification.
